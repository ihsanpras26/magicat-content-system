# Magicat Threads Content System Design

Date: 2026-09-09
Status: Approved in chat; pending written-spec review

## 1. Purpose

Build a practical Threads marketing system for Magicat (`@magicat.ai`) that:

- attracts beginner creators through useful, original AI experiments;
- uses Google Flow video results as the main discovery format;
- converts qualified interest into direct-message conversations;
- sells Gemini AI Pro 18-month activations for Rp49.000;
- creates a second product line from Flow prompts and workflows; and
- stores strategy, drafts, prompts, sales scripts, and performance data in an Obsidian-compatible vault.

The account must feel like a curious friend sharing experiments, not a product catalogue or an automated content account.

## 2. Confirmed Business Context

### Brand

- Brand: Magicat
- Username: `@magicat.ai`
- Instagram and Threads accounts are already active.
- Primary audience: beginner creators.
- Brand voice: a friend who experiments, reports what happened, and helps others try it.

### Product

- Product: Gemini AI Pro benefit for 18 months.
- Launch price: Rp49.000.
- Activation occurs on the buyer's own Gmail account.
- Magicat does not request the buyer's password or OTP.
- The benefit originates from the official Google and Jio program.
- Magicat has stated that it has written resale confirmation and written consent from the relevant Jio-number owners.
- The related Jio plans are maintained for the promised benefit period.
- Magicat provides replacement coverage if an eligible benefit ends before the promised period.
- Sales conversations happen through Threads or Instagram DM.
- Payment happens through QRIS.

The written resale confirmation, owner consent, and operating records remain internal. They do not need to be published, but Magicat must retain them while selling the product.

- "Benefit berasal dari program resmi Google x Jio."
- "Aktif di Gmail kamu sendiri."
- "Tanpa meminta password atau OTP."
- "Masa benefit 18 bulan sejak aktivasi," provided the operating record confirms the activation date.
- "Garansi penggantian," when accompanied by or linked to the written coverage terms.
- Specific included features that are confirmed immediately before publication.

### Prohibited or Unverified Claims

- "Magicat adalah partner resmi Google/Jio," unless Magicat has direct authorization to describe itself that way.
- "Akun resmi Magicat dari Google."
- "Pasti aktif tanpa risiko."
- "Lifetime."
- Fixed feature limits that have not been checked immediately before publication.
- Artificial scarcity, fake testimonials, fake sales counts, fake deadlines, or fabricated stock numbers.
- Any instruction asking buyers to send a password, OTP, recovery code, or session cookie.

## 6. Content Strategy

Use the hybrid value-to-sale approach:

| Pillar | Share | Job |
|---|---:|---|
| Flow experiments and showcases | 30% | Earn attention with visual proof |
| Practical prompts and workflows | 25% | Deliver reusable value and create prompt demand |
| Beginner AI education | 20% | Build understanding and authority |
| Conversation and opinion | 10% | Generate useful replies and audience insight |
| Product and customer proof | 15% | Convert trust into qualified DMs |

Weekly operating cadence:

- five text, image, or carousel posts;
- two Flow showcase videos;
- one direct sales post;
- one integrated soft-sell post;
- ten to fifteen useful replies per day in relevant conversations; and
- one weekly review of performance and customer questions.

If seven weekly publications reduce quality, reduce the total to five while retaining two videos.

## 7. Threads Writing System

### Voice Rules

- Use `aku` for experiments and personal observations.
- Use `Magicat` for policy, coverage, and support statements.
- Start with a concrete result, surprise, mistake, or observation.
- Keep one main idea per post.
- Use varied sentence length and natural Indonesian.
- Include details that came from the real experiment.
- Admit failed attempts when useful.
- Prefer specific language over generic superlatives.
- Do not force a question onto every post.
- Avoid excessive emoji, title case, jargon, and rigid three-point structures.
- Manually edit every AI-assisted draft using facts from the actual asset or experiment.

### Disallowed Style Patterns

- "Di era digital seperti sekarang..."
- "Apakah kamu siap meningkatkan kreativitasmu?"
- "Auto viral," "auto cuan," or unsupported income promises.
- Repeated `Ketik MAU` calls with no concrete delivery.
- Manufactured urgency or social proof.

### Flow Showcase Structure

1. Show the strongest result first.
2. Give one-sentence context.
3. Explain one production problem.
4. Reveal one prompt or workflow insight.
5. Invite a meaningful response or DM action.

Example:

> Nyoba bikin iklan parfum seolah direkam di gurun, padahal semuanya dibuat di Flow.
>
> Bagian paling susah ternyata bukan produknya. Kamera selalu terasa terlalu cepat.
>
> Setelah "slow controlled dolly-in" ditaruh sebelum deskripsi objek, gerakannya jauh lebih tenang.
>
> Kalau kamu punya produk sendiri, sebutkan produknya. Aku coba bantu cari arah prompt-nya.

## 8. CTA System

Use a CTA only when it matches the post's job.

| Goal | CTA pattern |
|---|---|
| Conversation | Ask which detail, trade-off, or result the reader prefers |
| Audience research | Ask for the reader's product or niche |
| Free prompt delivery | Ask readers to DM `FLOW` for a resource that is actually ready |
| Prompt sale | Ask readers to DM `PROMPT` to see the pack contents before purchasing |
| Gemini sale | Ask readers to DM `GEMINI` for benefits, activation steps, and coverage |
| Safety objection | Ask readers to DM `AMAN` for the no-password flow and coverage terms |

Avoid empty engagement bait. A keyword comment or DM must produce a promised resource, explanation, or next step.

## 10. Coverage Policy Design

The implementation must create a customer-facing coverage document with these fields:

- coverage period: 18 months from recorded activation;
- covered event: benefit ending early for reasons outside the buyer's control;
- remedy: restore access or provide a replacement benefit;
- first-response target: one business day;
- resolution target: three business days where replacement inventory is available;
- evidence: order ID, Gmail address used for activation, activation date, and screenshot of current subscription state;
- exclusions: buyer policy violations, sale or transfer of the activated account, country or payment-profile manipulation by the buyer, loss of access to the Gmail account, and voluntary cancellation or account deletion;
- fallback if restoration and replacement are both impossible: refund the value of the unused whole months on a prorated basis using `Rp49.000 / 18`, rounded up to the nearest rupiah. A partial month already started counts as used.

The final customer-facing policy must describe the order of remedies as restoration, replacement, then prorated refund. Promotional content may use "garansi 18 bulan" only when that policy is available to the buyer before payment.

## 11. Thirty-Day Editorial Framework

### Week 1: Introduction and Discovery

- Why Magicat exists.
- Flow cinematic product-ad showcase.
- Three real prompt-video mistakes.
- Conversation about what makes AI video feel artificial.
- Before-and-after prompt repair.
- Flow camera-movement breakdown.
- Soft sell: the tool used for the week's experiments.

### Week 2: Education and Trust

- Free Gemini versus AI Pro for beginner creators.
- Flow showcase for a small-business product.
- Fill-in-the-blank prompt template.
- Failed result and root cause.
- Idea-to-video mini workflow.
- Simple prompt versus structured prompt.
- Direct Gemini offer focused on safe activation.

### Week 3: Proof and Community

- Answer the most common DM question.
- Create a video from a follower's idea.
- Break a prompt into subject, action, camera, light, and constraints.
- Ask the audience to select the next product niche.
- Publish an approved, redacted activation testimonial.
- Publish the follower-inspired result.
- Direct Gemini offer using the `GEMINI` keyword.

### Week 4: Conversion and Learning

- Explain who does and does not need AI Pro.
- Publish the month's best Flow result.
- Share a free mini prompt pack.
- Compare a manual workflow with an AI-assisted workflow.
- Report what Magicat learned after thirty days.
- Showcase plus paid-prompt offer.
- Gemini offer with a concise coverage summary.

Implementation expands this framework into thirty ready-to-edit notes. Flow captions contain asset placeholders where facts must come from the actual generated video; they must not fabricate experiment details.

### Workflow

`Idea -> Draft -> factual review -> human voice edit -> Ready -> Published -> metrics -> learning -> Recycle`

Content cannot move to `Ready` unless:

- product claims match `01 Brand/Claims Allowed.md`;
- real experiment details replace placeholders;
- the CTA has a real fulfillment path;
- media ownership or permission is confirmed; and
- spelling, price, dates, and links are checked.

## 13. Metrics

Track:

- views per post;
- replies per 1,000 views;
- profile visits;
- DMs by keyword;
- qualified DMs;
- DM-to-payment conversion;
- successful activations;
- median activation time;
- coverage claims;
- replacement rate; and
- sales attributed to each post and format.

Thirty-day operating targets:

- 28 to 30 publications;
- eight Flow showcases;
- at least 20 qualified DMs;
- 10% to 25% DM-to-payment conversion as an initial learning range;
- three to five approved activation testimonials;
- zero requests for passwords or OTPs; and
- all recurring customer questions captured as content ideas.

These are planning targets, not public performance claims.

## 15. Acceptance Criteria

The system is complete when:

- Obsidian can open the workspace as a usable Markdown vault;
- every day in the first thirty days has a content note and clear purpose;
- all promotional drafts use only permitted claims;
- all Flow-dependent drafts visibly identify facts that must be supplied from the real asset;
- DM scripts cover inquiry, qualification, payment, activation, support, and testimonial permission;
- the coverage fallback is explicit;
- no workflow requests buyer credentials or OTPs;
- the metrics template can connect a publication to DMs and sales; and
- a fresh reader can understand the offer, its source, its limitations, and the next action without prior conversation context.
